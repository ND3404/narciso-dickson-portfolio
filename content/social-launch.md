I launched a new professional portfolio for my work at the intersection of construction project management, project controls, data analytics, business intelligence, and responsible AI leadership.

My goal is to show how project leadership and analytical discipline can work together: clearer reporting, earlier risk visibility, better executive conversations, and AI-assisted workflows that still require qualified human review.
